PM Modi, Meloni in Rome: Car ride, dinner and Colosseum visit ahead of talks

Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni welcomed Prime Minister Narendra Modi in Rome ahead of key bilateral talks between India and Italy. The two leaders shared a warm interaction during a car ride through the city and later attended a private dinner meeting. PM Modi also visited the historic Colosseum, one of Rome’s most iconic landmarks, before the official discussions. The meeting is expected to focus on trade, technology, defence cooperation and global partnerships between the two countries.
