PSG retains Champions League title after edging past Arsenal on penalties

Paris Saint-Germain defended their European crown in dramatic fashion at the Puskás Aréna in Budapest.

Paris Saint-Germain successfully defended their European crown in a dramatic Champions League final at the Puskás Aréna in Budapest. After a tense 1-1 draw through 120 minutes of football, the French champions held their nerve to win 4-3 on penalties against a resilient Arsenal side.

The victory marks PSG's second consecutive Champions League triumph and cements their status as the dominant force in European football. Kylian Mbappé once again proved decisive, scoring the crucial penalty in the shootout after a man-of-the-match performance during regulation time.

Arsenal, appearing in their first final since 2006, pushed the defending champions all the way. Bukayo Saka gave the Gunners a first-half lead before Mbappé equalized in the 67th minute. Despite creating several chances in extra time, neither side could find a winner, setting up the dramatic penalty shootout.

For Arsenal, it was a case of so near yet so far. Mikel Arteta's young side had defied expectations to reach the final, knocking out Real Madrid and Bayern Munich along the way. Goalkeeper David Raya, whose journey from non-league football to the biggest stage in club football has captured hearts worldwide, saved two penalties but could not prevent PSG from lifting the trophy.
